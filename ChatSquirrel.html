ChatSquirrel Privacy Policy

隐私政策 · 生效日期 Effective date: November 21, 2026

中文 English

中文

ChatSquirrel（下称“本扩展”）是一款浏览器扩展，用于把你在 ChatGPT、Gemini、DeepSeek 或 Grok 上当前打开的对话导出为 PDF、Word、Markdown 或 TXT 文件。本政策说明本扩展处理哪些信息。

1. 我们处理的信息

在你的设备上处理、不上传到我们的服务器：

  • 当前对话的文字、图片和标题，仅用于生成你保存到本机的文件。
  • 这些网站为加载该对话而发出的请求头和会话标识，仅保存在本次浏览器会话的内存中，用于重新获取这段对话。
  • 界面语言、是否已显示评价提示、登录令牌，以及当日剩余导出次数。这些保存在扩展的本地存储中。

你选择注册或登录时，会发送到我们的服务器（https://aichat-export-backend.jiangwenchao6666.workers.dev）：

  • 电子邮箱、验证码、密码。注册时如果你填写了邀请码，邀请码也会发送。
  • 你的时区，用于按自然日计算导出额度。
  • 一次导出记录：文件类型（PDF、Word、Markdown 或 TXT）和站点名称（ChatGPT、Gemini、DeepSeek 或 Grok）。不含对话内容。

未登录使用时：

  • 本扩展根据浏览器特征生成本机标识。特征包括用户代理、语言、平台、CPU 核心数、设备内存、屏幕尺寸、时区、Canvas 绘制结果和显卡渲染器信息。这些原始特征只在本地计算，服务器只收到计算后的标识（以 g_ 开头的一串字符）。
  • 该标识、时区、文件类型和站点名称会发送到服务器，用于统计免费导出次数。

2. 我们如何使用这些信息

  • 生成并保存你要求导出的文件。
  • 创建和维护你的账号，发送验证码，完成登录、重置密码和额度查询。
  • 按日统计导出次数，执行免费次数和会员额度。
  • 记住你的语言和评价提示状态。

我们不出售个人信息，不用这些信息投放广告，也不上传对话内容。

3. 信息保存多久

  • 对话内容不写入我们的服务器。导出文件由你自己保存在电脑上。
  • 登录令牌、语言、本机标识和额度缓存保存在扩展本地存储中，直到你退出登录、清除扩展数据或卸载扩展。
  • 账号资料和导出次数记录保存在我们的服务器上，直到你要求删除账号。

4. 共享

我们不向第三方出售或出租你的信息。下列情况除外：

  • 你导出时，本扩展会以你在该网站已登录的身份，向 ChatGPT、Gemini、DeepSeek 或 Grok 请求当前对话。这些请求受该网站自己的隐私政策约束。
  • 账号与额度服务托管在 Cloudflare Workers 上。Cloudflare 会为提供托管服务而处理相应的网络请求。
  • 法律要求时，我们可能披露必要信息。

5. 你的选择

  • 可以不注册，以访客身份使用有限的每日导出次数。
  • 可以在扩展中退出登录。退出后本地登录令牌会被删除。
  • 可以卸载扩展。卸载会移除保存在扩展本地存储中的数据。
  • 可以来信要求删除服务器上的账号、邮箱和导出记录。

6. 儿童

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

7. 政策变更

我们可能更新本政策。更新后的文本会发布在本页面，并修改页首的生效日期。若变更会实质影响我们收集或使用信息的方式，我们会在扩展或本页面作出说明。

8. 联系我们

如需查询、更正或删除你的信息，请发送邮件至：your-email@example.com

English

ChatSquirrel is a browser extension that exports the conversation you currently have open on ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, or Grok to a PDF, Word, Markdown, or TXT file. This policy describes the information the extension handles.

1. Information we process

Processed on your device and not uploaded to our server:

  • The text, images, and title of the current conversation, used only to build the file you save on your computer.
  • Request headers and the conversation id those sites use to load that conversation. They stay in memory for the browser session and are used only to fetch that conversation again.
  • Your interface language, whether the review prompt has been shown, your sign-in token, and the remaining export count for the day. These are kept in the extension’s local storage.

Sent to our server at https://aichat-export-backend.jiangwenchao6666.workers.dev if you register or sign in:

  • Your email address, verification code, and password. An invite code is sent only if you enter one.
  • Your time zone, so daily export limits follow your calendar day.
  • An export record containing the file type (PDF, Word, Markdown, or TXT) and the site name (ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, or Grok). The conversation itself is not included.

If you use the extension without an account:

  • The extension creates a local identifier from browser traits: user agent, language, platform, CPU count, device memory, screen size, time zone, a canvas drawing, and the graphics renderer. Those traits stay on your device. The server receives only the resulting identifier, a string starting with g_.
  • That identifier, your time zone, the file type, and the site name are sent to count free exports.

2. How we use this information

  • To create and save the file you ask to export.
  • To create and maintain your account, send verification codes, and handle sign-in, password reset, and quota checks.
  • To count exports per day and apply free and member limits.
  • To remember your language and review-prompt state.

We do not sell personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not upload conversation content.

3. How long we keep it

  • Conversation content is not written to our server. Exported files remain wherever you save them.
  • The sign-in token, language, local identifier, and quota cache stay in extension storage until you sign out, clear the extension’s data, or uninstall it.
  • Account details and export-count records stay on our server until you ask us to delete the account.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only in these cases:

  • When you export, the extension requests the current conversation from ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, or Grok using your existing sign-in on that site. Those requests are covered by that site’s own privacy policy.
  • The account and quota service is hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare processes the network requests required to run that service.
  • We may disclose information when the law requires it.

5. Your choices

  • You can use a limited number of daily exports without registering.
  • You can sign out in the extension. Signing out deletes the local sign-in token.
  • You can uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes data stored in the extension’s local storage.
  • You can email us to delete your account, email address, and export records from our server.

6. Children

This extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The new text will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we collect or use information, we will note it in the extension or on this page.

8. Contact

To access, correct, or delete your information, email: your-email@example.com
